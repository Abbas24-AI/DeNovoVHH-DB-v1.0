Help & methods

What this database is — and is not

DeNovoVHH-DB catalogues single-domain antibodies (VHH / nanobodies) across the full continuum from computational design to experimental validation. Every field carries its source and a data-status label. Crucially, the database preserves the distinction:

designed ≠ tested ≠ binder ≠ functional ≠ therapeutic. A record's presence does not imply it binds, works, or is a drug — only what the evidence located actually shows.

How records are built

  1. Sequences, targets, affinities and CDRs are ingested from the ANDD dataset (Zenodo 18151718, CC-BY-4.0) and cross-referenced source databases.
  2. Every PDB entry is enriched live from RCSB PDB (method, resolution, title, deposition, linked PMID/DOI).
  3. Targets are annotated from UniProt; publications and mined design methods from Europe PMC.
  4. Physicochemical properties are calculated from sequence with Biopython and labelled as such.
  5. CDRs are taken from the source curation; framework boundaries are derived by exact substring matching (IMGT numbering tools were not forced where unavailable).
